# Visual Audit Findings

## Primary dashboard screenshot

The dashboard is a strong visual proof point: it communicates a calm, operational command center with a left navigation, search, live status indicator, and KPI cards for revenue, active members, class attendance, and member satisfaction. The screenshot supports claims around visibility, analytics, and daily operational control.

The UI is clean and legible, with a restrained blue/green/purple/gold accent system and generous whitespace. The screenshot is highly suitable for hero product proof, carousel ads, and feature-led landing sections.

The dashboard currently displays concrete-looking figures such as $45,280 revenue, 1,247 active members, 78% class attendance, and 4.7/5 satisfaction. These should be labeled as demo/example data or replaced with verified customer results before using them as public proof claims.

## Members screenshot

The members page visibly demonstrates member records, active/inactive status, last check-in dates, tags such as Loyal, High Performer, and At Risk, and row-level actions. This supports a retention-oriented story: operators can identify members needing attention instead of relying on memory or scattered messages.

The screenshot is useful for campaigns focused on member retention, engagement, churn prevention, and staff follow-up. It also reveals a potential marketing opportunity to explain the specific workflow from risk signal to outreach action, because the screenshot shows the signal but not the resulting recovery outcome.

## Shared visual implications

The strongest creative territory is operational clarity rather than generic fitness motivation. Recommended message themes are: one source of truth, see the whole gym at a glance, catch churn before cancellation, and replace spreadsheet chaos with a focused operating system.

The interface appears desktop-first and professional. Mobile campaign creatives should crop to the KPI cards or member-risk table rather than reproduce the full dashboard at unreadable scale.
