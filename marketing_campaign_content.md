# MTDRB / إدارة Campaign Content System

**Campaign platform:** Run the gym. Not the chaos.

**Audience:** Gym owners, studio founders, general managers, and operations leads in GCC/MENAT markets.

**Primary conversion:** Start a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

**Secondary conversion:** Book a 15-minute workflow review or product demo.

## Campaign concept

The launch campaign should contrast the visible calm of a live operating dashboard with the hidden chaos of scattered admin. The creative pattern is simple: show one recognizable operator problem, show the relevant MTDRB screen, and end with one clear next action.

The campaign should not pretend that software alone creates business growth. It should show how better visibility helps a team make faster, more consistent decisions. The product screenshots are the hero assets because they demonstrate the experience better than abstract stock photography.

MTDRB brings members, classes, trainers, attendance, billing, and analytics into one operating rhythm. Built for bilingual teams across the Gulf and MENAT region. **Run the gym. ### Post 2: retention

**English:** The member who goes quiet rarely cancels all at once. The signal usually appears earlier: fewer check-ins, an overdue renewal, or a missed class pattern. MTDRB helps your team see the signal and organize the follow-up before it gets buried.

**Arabic:** العضو الذي يبتعد لا يُلغي عضويته عادةً فجأة. تظهر الإشارة مبكراً: حضور أقل، تجديد متأخر، أو نمط غياب عن الحصص. تساعد MTDRB فريقك على رؤية الإشارة وتنظيم المتابعة قبل أن تضيع بين المهام.

### Post 3: scheduling

**English:** A full class is good. A double-booked class is not. MTDRB connects schedules, capacity, bookings, waitlists, and attendance so your team can run the day with fewer surprises.

**Arabic:** امتلاء الحصة خبر جيد. أما الحجز المزدوج فليس كذلك. تربط MTDRB بين الجداول والسعة والحجوزات وقوائم الانتظار والحضور حتى يدير فريقك اليوم بمفاجآت أقل.

### Post 4: regional readiness

**English:** Localization is more than translating a button. It is the language your team thinks in, the direction your interface reads in, and the regional workflows your business expects. MTDRB is built for English, Arabic, RTL, and region-oriented operations from day one.

**Arabic:** التوطين ليس مجرد ترجمة زر. إنه اللغة التي يفكر بها فريقك، واتجاه الواجهة الذي يقرأه، والعمليات الإقليمية التي يتوقعها عملك. صُممت MTDRB للإنجليزية والعربية وRTL والعمليات الملائمة للمنطقة منذ اليوم الأول.

## Short-form video scripts

### Script A: “One glance”

Open on a phone camera pointed at a chaotic desk: spreadsheet, chat notifications, printed schedule. Cut to the MTDRB dashboard. Voiceover: “What if the first question of the morning had one answer? Members. Attendance. Revenue. Classes. One glance.” End card: “MTDRB. Run the gym. Not the chaos.”

### Script B: “The quiet member”

Open on the members list. Highlight an inactive or at-risk tag. Voiceover: “The member you lose is often the member you stopped seeing. MTDRB helps your team spot inactivity and organize the next follow-up.” End card: “More renewals. Fewer surprises.” Do not claim that the software itself increases renewals until customer evidence exists.

### Script C: “Built local”

Show the language switcher, Arabic interface, and RTL layout. Voiceover: “A bilingual team should not have to work around an English-only system. Switch languages, read right to left, and keep the same operating rhythm.” End card: “Built local. Not translated.”

## Email sequence

### Email 1 — Welcome

**Subject:** Your gym, one operating rhythm

You do not need another tool that creates more work. MTDRB brings the daily essentials together: members, classes, trainers, attendance, billing, and analytics. Start by adding one member, one class, and one team member. Then open the dashboard and see your operation in one place.

**CTA:** Start your trial.

### Email 2 — First value

**Subject:** Your first 15 minutes in MTDRB

In your first session, complete three actions: create or import a member, schedule a class, and review the dashboard. Those three steps are enough to make the product feel real in your workflow. You can refine branding, branches, and settings after the basics are working.

**CTA:** Complete setup.

### Email 3 — Retention workflow

**Subject:** Find the members who need attention

A member list is useful. A member list that helps your team decide what to do next is better. Use check-in history, status, tags, and analytics to organize follow-up. Start with one small list: members who have not visited recently.

**CTA:** Review members.

### Email 4 — Team adoption

**Subject:** Give every role the view it needs

Owners need business visibility. Employees need reliable member and class workflows. Trainers need schedules and assignments. MTDRB’s role-aware workspace helps your team work from one system without giving everyone the same access.

**CTA:** Invite your team.

### Email 5 — Conversion

**Subject:** Keep the calm after your trial

Your trial is designed to help you test MTDRB against the way your gym actually runs. Review the workflows your team used most, note the manual work you replaced, and choose the plan that fits your operation. If payment processing is not yet configured for your account, contact the team for next-step guidance rather than assuming automatic billing is live.

**CTA:** Book a workflow review.

## Creative briefs

### Hero static

Use the existing dashboard screenshot on a light background with a compact dark text block. Headline: “Your whole gym. One glance.” Supporting line: “Members, classes, billing, and performance in one operating rhythm.” CTA: “Start 14-day trial.” Add a small bilingual badge: “English / العربية · RTL ready.” Use sample-data labeling if the screenshot figures are not real customer results.

### Member retention carousel

Frame 1: “The member who goes quiet is easy to miss.” Frame 2: show the members list and inactive/at-risk tags. Frame 3: “Turn a signal into a follow-up.” Frame 4: “More renewals. Fewer surprises.” Frame 5: “Try the workflow in MTDRB.” Keep the claim capability-led until verified outcomes are available.

### Operator checklist lead magnet

Title: “The 5-Minute Gym Operations Audit.” Sections: Can you see active members instantly? Can staff find the next class without asking? Can you identify overdue renewals? Can you see revenue and attendance trends? Can Arabic- and English-speaking staff use the same system? The final page should invite the reader to test MTDRB against the gaps they found.

## Hashtag and distribution guidance

Use a small, stable set of relevant tags rather than a long block. Suggested English tags are `#GymManagement`, `#FitnessBusiness`, `#GymOwners`, `#FitnessStudio`, and `#GymOperations`. Suggested Arabic tags can be tested with native operators and partners rather than translated mechanically. Every post should include a region or operator context in the copy, not only in hashtags.

## Content production rules

Use real product screens, real workflows, and clear before/after explanations. Use bilingual captions for every product video that targets the region. Keep visual text large enough for mobile. Never publish invented customer logos, invented testimonials, unverified revenue or retention lifts, or a claim that payment processing is live if the account is still using trial entitlement activation.
